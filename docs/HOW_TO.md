# How-To Guide — Marquee

Short, task-based answers to "how do I…?". For the big-picture setup story (how
a brokerage and its agents fit together), see [`ONBOARDING.md`](ONBOARDING.md).

Everything here is point-and-click — no technical knowledge needed. Tip: use
Chrome or Safari; voice dictation needs one of those.

---

## Get started
1. Go to the app and click **Sign up**.
2. Create your account — you land in the **Studio** (dashboard).
3. The first thing to do is set your **market area** and **brand** (below) so
   everything you generate is tailored to you.

## Set the area you sell in (and your MLS)
This tailors every post, email, and SMS — the towns it names, the local angle,
the hashtags — and sets which state listing imports pull from.
1. **Company → Brand & documents** → scroll to **Market area & MLS**.
2. Pick your **State** and type your **County**.
3. (Optional) Add a **Region** (e.g. "Jersey Shore") and your **key towns**
   (comma-separated) — the AI names these for hyperlocal posts.
4. Type **Your MLS** (start typing to pick from common ones, or enter your own).
5. Click **Save brand kit**.

> Admins set this for the whole company. If it's greyed out with "Locked by org",
> your brokerage manages it for you.

## Set your brand (colors, logo, disclaimer)
1. **Company → Brand & documents**.
2. Set **primary / secondary / accent** colors, upload your **logo** (light- and
   dark-background versions), and add your **disclaimer / brokerage line**.
3. Fast path: click **Import a brand guide** and upload your brand PDF — Claude
   pulls out your colors, fonts, and disclaimer. Review, then **Save**.
4. Your personal details (headshot, name, license, phone) live under **Profile**.

## Make the app show your brand (white-label) — brokerages
1. **Company → Brand & documents** → check **White-label the app**.
2. Upload a **dark-background logo** (it shows in the sidebar) and a
   **light-background logo** (for the top bar on phones).
3. **Save.** The app now shows your logo and name instead of "Marquee".

## Create a post
1. **Create**.
2. Pick a **type** (Just Sold, New Listing, Open House, Market Stat, …).
3. Add the listing details (or import one — below), and **upload photos** or turn
   on **AI image** to generate the graphic.
4. (Optional) Describe it in one line and we fill in the rest; you can **dictate**
   with the mic.
5. Click **Generate**. Review the graphic + caption, then save it to a campaign or
   schedule it.

## Pull a listing from the MLS (and reuse it)
1. On **Create**, click **Import from MLS**.
2. Search by **address**, **ZIP**, or **"City, ST"**. (A city with no state uses
   your market-area state.)
3. Click a result to **fill the form**, or click **Save** to keep it.
4. Reuse a saved one anytime: **My listings** → pick it. Editing a field by hand
   treats it as a new property.
5. Delete a saved listing from the **My listings** list (the ✕).

> Needs your brokerage to have MLS lookup enabled. No photos come from the MLS —
> upload your own or use the AI-image option.

## Write an email or text
1. **Create** → switch the channel to **Email** or **SMS** at the top.
2. Pick the type, add details, **Generate**.
3. Emails include your brokerage disclaimer automatically; texts stay short.

## Turn a post into a reel
1. Generate a post whose format is a **Reel** (e.g. New Listing, Market Stat) —
   you'll see a **Reel script / shot list**.
2. Click **🎬 Assemble reel**. You get a **storyboard**: each scene with its
   on-screen text, what to film, an assigned photo, and timing.
3. **Download shot list** to film it yourself, or — if your workspace has reel
   rendering enabled — a finished **MP4** appears to download and post.

## Plan a month of content
1. **Calendar** → **Plan 30 days**.
2. Adjust the **content mix** sliders (educational / community / listings / social
   proof / personal brand) and the number of posts.
3. Review drafts in the queue; click any post to edit the caption, reschedule, or
   approve/publish.

## Publish to social (or export)
1. **Profile → Connections** → connect each platform (Instagram, Facebook,
   LinkedIn, X).
2. Schedule posts from the Calendar queue.
3. If a platform isn't connected/approved yet, use **manual export** — download
   the image and copy the caption. Same queue, nothing lost.

## Capture leads
1. **Leads** → create a capture form / landing page (or an open-house sign-in).
2. Share the **link** or **QR code**.
3. Leads land in the **Leads** hub; move them through new → contacted → qualified
   → won/lost, and convert a lead into a **deal** to track revenue.

## Send feedback or request a feature (and track it)
1. Click **Send support & feedback** at the bottom of any page.
2. Choose **Report a problem**, **Share feedback**, or **Request a feature**; add
   a subject + details (and a reply-to email if you'd like a response).
3. Track where it stands under **View your requests** (`/feedback`): Received →
   Under review → Planned → Shipped.

## Invite your agents (brokerage admins)
1. **Company → Agents** → invite by email.
2. They inherit your company brand automatically and just add their own listings,
   photos, and personal details.
3. **Lock** any brand fields you don't want agents to change (colors, logo,
   market area, etc.).

## Manage your subscription
1. **Company → Subscription** → choose a plan and check out.
2. Manage card, invoices, and plan changes from the **Customer Portal** link there.

---

### Troubleshooting (quick)
- **"Import from MLS" says it isn't set up** — your brokerage hasn't enabled MLS
  lookup yet; ask your admin.
- **Posts won't publish** — the platform may still be pending approval; use manual
  export in the meantime, or reconnect the account under Profile → Connections.
- **A brand field is greyed out** — your org admin has locked it.
- **Wrong town/area in posts** — update **Company → Brand → Market area & MLS**.
