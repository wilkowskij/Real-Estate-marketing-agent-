## 1. Company

**NorthStar Realty** is a boutique brokerage serving **Travis County, Texas** —
Austin and the surrounding hill-country towns (Westlake, Lakeway, Bee Cave,
Dripping Springs, Pflugerville). We help move-up buyers and first-time sellers
find their footing in one of the country's fastest-changing markets.

- **Market area:** Travis County, TX (region: Texas Hill Country)
- **Key towns:** Austin, Westlake, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, Pflugerville
- **MLS:** Austin/Central Texas Realty Information Service (ACTRIS)

## 2. Logo

The mark is a four-point **north star** paired with the NORTHSTAR wordmark.

- **`northstar-logo-light.svg`** — for light backgrounds (navy ink).
- **`northstar-logo-dark.svg`** — for dark backgrounds (paper ink). Use this one
  for the app sidebar when white-labeling.
- Keep clear space of at least the star's width on all sides. Don't recolor the
  star or stretch the wordmark.

## 3. Color palette

Our palette is calm and editorial — deep teal grounded by warm sand, with a
single terracotta accent reserved for calls to action.

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Primary** | Teal Pine | `#15564E` |
| **Secondary** | Warm Sand | `#EFE7D6` |
| **Accent** | Terracotta | `#D9694C` |
| Ink (text) | Slate | `#22302E` |
| Paper (bg) | Bone | `#FBF8F1` |

Terracotta is the **call-to-action** color — buttons, links, "Schedule a tour".
Use it sparingly so it stays loud.

## 4. Typography

- **Headings:** Cormorant Garamond (serif, high contrast).
- **Body:** Inter (humanist sans, set at a comfortable 16px).

Headlines are short and confident; body copy is generous and easy to scan.

## 5. Voice & tone

Confident, warm, and genuinely local. We sound like a trusted neighbor who knows
the school zones, the commute, and which street floods — never like a billboard.
Lead with something specific and true; end by inviting a real conversation.

## 6. Legal

> NorthStar Realty, LLC. Each office is independently owned and operated. Equal
> Housing Opportunity. Information deemed reliable but not guaranteed.

Use the line above verbatim as the disclaimer on marketing materials.
