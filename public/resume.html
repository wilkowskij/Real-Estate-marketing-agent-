The Story

I spent four years as a diesel mechanic in the US Army, including a deployment to Iraq in 2008–2009. In that environment, there's no room for "it should work" — equipment either functions or people are at risk. That standard — does it actually work? — is the lens I carry into every product I build.

"I don't just manage roadmaps. I write the API specs, prototype in Figma, author the data flow diagrams, and — when the moment calls for it — ship the code myself."

After transitioning from the military, I moved through operations and field service leadership before landing in product management — where strategy, execution, and customer reality all converge. Over 15+ years spanning transportation, automotive aftermarket, and enterprise tech, I've learned to sit comfortably at the intersection of product vision and hands-on technical delivery.

At CDK Global, I led an AI-driven warranty automation platform that cut processing time from one week to one hour and launched a new high-growth revenue stream on AWS — in four months. I wrote the technical specs and API/data flow diagrams engineering built from, built working prototypes to validate before investment, and directed a 15-person cross-functional team from zero to launch.

Today, I'm also a builder. I shipped Marquee — a live multi-tenant SaaS where four Claude-powered AI agents collaborate to generate personalized, compliant marketing content, with Stripe billing, social OAuth publishing, and 55+ automated tests. Not a side project. Production software.
